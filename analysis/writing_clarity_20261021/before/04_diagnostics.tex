\subsection{Operational and Contract Diagnostics}\label{sec:diagnostics}
The construction results above concern declared task conditions. Historical
records and corrected contracts explain why a missing recovery cannot always
be assigned to cryptanalytic failure. Their operational populations and weaker
reconstruction support remain separate from the matched and prospective studies.

\paragraph{Historical Transcript Census}\label{sec:historical}
A census of 74 retained session-end records (V2/V3 byte-identical copies
counted once) yields 42 accepted, 2 rejected, and 30 no-verdict (R tier); the
per-task split and the service-secret claims are in the supplement and are
recomputed by the artifact's study replay. The census
is not pooled with the prospective or legacy cohorts; earlier tables reused run
numbers across tables with no reconciled common membership, so no unified
historical attack rate is reported.

\paragraph{Legacy Agent Loop}\label{sec:legacy}
The legacy real-agent loop (EXP-014) schedules 54 sessions. Twenty-eight are
provider-unavailable (retained, never counted as failures); 3 are relay-invalid;
23 are valid, of which 10 succeed. The yield is 10/23 $=$ 43.5\% on the valid
subset and 10/51 $=$ 19.6\% provider-inclusive (R tier); the population
partition is in the supplement. Terminal statuses: 10 SUCCESS, 12 AGENT\_GIVEUP,
4 ORACLE\_FAIL. Canonical session statuses are reported separately from the
legacy \texttt{no\_claim}/\texttt{failure} labels: provider-unavailable and
relay-invalid rows are excluded under the stated rules, and their field-level
mapping stays in the run-level audit rather than becoming a V4 denominator.

Per-task: RC4 8/12, ChaCha20 2/3, CBC 0/4, ECDSA 0/4. Clock drift across 51
paired sessions has median $\approx$0.049\,ms; 4 rows exceed 1\,ms (max
$\approx$1{,}301\,ms) and 18 exceed 0.1\,ms; the strict timing-admissible subset
is 19 sessions. Agent-session medians ($T_M$, legacy): RC4 8.37\,s ($n{=}8$),
ChaCha20 3.57\,s ($n{=}2$). These are session spans over the successful
sessions and are not the $T_{op}$ operation spans of Section~\ref{sec:floor}.

\paragraph{Failure Anatomy}\label{sec:failures}
Across 230 attempts in the continuation cohorts, 135 do not reach success:
48 tool failures + 23 harness failures (52.6\% combined), 45 oracle failures
(33.3\%), 13 silent refusals (9.6\%), and 6 timeout/give-up; a separate
545-record census gives the same ordering, and two further negative arms are
retained (a clean-primary CBC arm, 15 records, all tool/give-up/timeout
non-successes; and a contract-v3 arm, 12/12 provider-unavailable, excluded
under the PU rule). Tool and harness failures dominate the recorded
non-success labels, locating an operational bottleneck rather than resolving
the relation in those runs.

\paragraph{Independent Revalidation (Checkpoint 009)}\label{sec:revalidation}
A separately registered revalidation arm re-queries three families under
named, unnamed, and underdetermined opportunity statements with 10 matched
seeds each: for each of RC4, ECDSA, and XOR, 0/10 recoveries named, 0/10
unnamed, and 10/10 correct abstentions when observations are structurally
insufficient---90 calls, zero provider failures, \$0.1389 estimated cost.
The retained records mark the prior report on this arm invalid (placeholder
prompts, arm-ID mismatch) and keep it as provenance. Validation confirms
independent local fixture recoverability with no verifier truth in the
prompts: the bounded reading is inference/construction failure under this
contract, not cryptographic non-identifiability (R tier). A companion
output-contract arm (9 calls, same public instances) parses 4/9 strict
single-object JSON responses yet finds 0 oracle recoveries: format compliance
alone does not build a model-to-oracle path.

\paragraph{Pilot and Halted Campaigns}\label{sec:pilot}
An E4 pilot rescores 20 historical attempts: 1 operational success (1/3 on
the rescored subset). The E2/W3 continuation campaign halts at 33/270---a
fail-closed non-result, not a rate. Continuations EXP-024/025/026 (230-session
and related cohorts) are separate censuses, never pooled with each other or
with the V4 cohort.


\paragraph{Recorded Refusal Diagnostics}\label{sec:safety}
The safety cost transfer SCT $=(1-\mathrm{SRR})/(1+\log_{10}(\mathrm{TTE}_M+1))$
gives Claw Code SCT 0.671 against an aligned Gemma4 configuration at 0.150, a
$4.5\times$ reduction (R tier, $n{=}5$/model, exploratory). This is a response
study --- a generated answer is not an executed exploit --- so it reports no
chain result. The composite is retained here as a historical diagnostic, not a
validated capability or risk measure. The artifact recomputes the refusal
rates; the supplement records the escalation ladder, the four silent
refusals, and the comparability limits.

These diagnostics qualify how construction outcomes are counted. Even an
accepted recovery program leaves a further question: does the target permit
the claimed security effect? The next section tests that requirement.
