## Scientific connection

The common question is **how an agent-generated cryptographic attack becomes a verified system compromise**. The paper follows construction, interaction with an exposed relation/interface, system outcome and checked recovery. Execution conditions and effort accompany that path.

- Agent transcripts establish generated programs, revision and accepted recovery.
- Supplied operations connect reference feasibility to observed agent completion or failure.
- Protocol-policy and leakage studies establish the system side of the path: what observable is available and what an endpoint admits.
- Escrow joins generated code, an explicit leakage interface and a candidate checked against a pre-session commitment; source inspection identifies the actual acquisition authority.
- Legacy delivery, query/relay histories and process calibration locate the work surrounding a final accepted operation.
- Reserved-truth checks, release context, provenance controls and offline replay establish which recovery, effort and acquisition statements are supported.

The experiments have complementary roles, with their original populations and units preserved. The connection does not require adding sessions, handshakes, timing observations, recovered bytes or diagnostic ratings into one total.
