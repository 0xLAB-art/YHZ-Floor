# Earlier ACTS multi-tier source: local reconciliation

This additive record reconciles the earlier ACTS multi-tier benchmark with the
current attack-centered program. It does not merge the two studies or treat
their denominators as comparable.

## Direct overlap excluded from novelty claims

The earlier source covers controlled metadata deprivation, blind cipher
identification, agentic tools, forced reasoning, deterministic heuristics,
classical ML, perturbation, feature ablations, and ML-KEM-768/classical
byte-signal comparisons. The current manuscript therefore makes no “first” or
unique claim for those constructs.

closure artifact. It is not pooled with current runs, not used as an
independent replication of the current attack construct, and not cited to
support a generic capability or novelty ranking.
