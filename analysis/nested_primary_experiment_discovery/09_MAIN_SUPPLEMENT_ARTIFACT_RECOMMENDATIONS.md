# Main, supplement, and artifact recommendations

These are evidence-placement recommendations only; no manuscript was changed.

## Main text candidates

- Keep only the C2b bounded mechanism statement if the paper needs one nested security result. It is about local hybrid TLS policy enforcement under the recorded client/server/build, not an ML-KEM or deployment claim.
- Do not promote D1, C2 original, or C2-T1 to a headline security contribution without stronger source-to-run and independent-reproduction evidence.

## Supplement candidates

- Add a compact discovered-family table listing C2, C2-T1, A1, B1, D1, C2b, fixed-operation rerun, and historical agent sessions with evidence grades and explicit non-pooling rules.
- Preserve A1’s positive/negative environment records and state that the contrast is descriptive, not causal.
- State that v2/v3 session trees are byte-identical copies and that the 74-session population is not 148 runs.
- Map the fixed-operation rerun to its operation-only claim and keep it separate from E2 agent outcomes.
- Map intentionally excluded families so a reviewer can distinguish selective scope from missing discovery.

## Artifact-only material

- Keep full C2b matrices, 90 traces, metadata, and SHA-256 pointers.
- Keep D1 JSON and cache-timing narrative, labelled with the lack of raw timing samples and independent rerun.
- Keep provider/no-claim/relay/harness-defect records, including 22 excluded rows.
- Keep both C2-T1 JSON paths or a manifest showing they are duplicate copies.

## Before stronger promotion

- raw sample/transcript retention linked to exact generator revision;
- immutable environment/build identity;
- independent rerun or controlled replication for D1/A1/C2 generality;
- explicit distinction between operation, agent, and historical populations;
- repository-to-paper manifest including intentionally excluded families.
