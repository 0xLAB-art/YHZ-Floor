\section{Limitations, Ethics, and Reproducibility}\label{sec:limits}

\textbf{Scope and identity.} Feasibility cells are small ($n=2$--$4$;
V4 $n=3$ per task). V4 fixes a mutable provider label; the post-freeze
studies use a different label and one experimenter. EXP-034 retains runtime
fingerprints and trace hashes but no immutable hosted-model image digest.
EXP-035 closes that link only for a network-disabled local OCI control.
V4 lacks token usage and HTTP attempt IDs; its 48-attempt/session bound
($8\times3\times2$) is a ceiling, not an observed count.

\textbf{Causal and measurement limits.} H1-R3's matched conclusion applies
to the tested representations, levels, margin, two families, and one route. The
powered $L3$ cells succeed in 124--126 of 128 sessions. This limits observable
improvement, not possible harm; a small discordant set and empirical bootstrap
coverage constrain interpretation. Lower-level R0 baselines do not uniformly
offer more headroom, and lower-level $R2$ contrasts remain inconclusive under
the frozen margin rule.
V4 and explicit-observation cohorts differ in token allowance, format,
instructions, parser, feedback, and observations; their contrast is
descriptive. The tier-E $59.5\times$ A1 $z$ contrast confounds build, timer,
preparation, and method. $TTE_M$ is quantized at 0.1\,s; eight successful V4
sessions lack raw harness endpoints, four legacy records breach the 1\,ms
gate, retries and D1 raw samples are unretained (D1 tier R), and E3 uses one host.

\textbf{System and external validity.} Table~\ref{tab:external-coverage}
distinguishes interface coverage, within-family transfer, independent case
construction, and policy-implementation agreement. None is an independent
benchmark replication of the admission effect. C2b/C2-T1 use one local
OpenSSL+liboqs build; C2-T1 lacks a completed post-edit handshake
($\texttt{r2\_verified=false}$). D1 uses a recompiled page-aligned victim,
not stock OpenSSL; A1 is host-specific. The escrow and release-audit
boundaries are stated with the studies they qualify. H1--H3 assess
release-evidence linkage, not system quality;
the six-release, nine-field audit's $\kappa$ measures internal reliability,
not system correctness. Attribution tests remain constructed cases under
trusted observers and signers; they estimate neither deployed failure
prevalence nor arbitrary-host soundness. Their bounded mechanized tests add
no attack numerator.

\textbf{Reproducibility and artifact mapping.} The public reviewer artifact
supports partial reconstruction. Its shipped records recompute the 852-session
census, paired contrasts and policy change, cross-arm identity, release and
campaign censuses, attribution-lineage cells, eight accepted prospective
submissions, the 74-session historical census, and fixed-executor 12/12
summaries. Related timing, cache, policy, wire-edit, configuration and safety
summaries remain linked to their records; arithmetic and integrity checks
do not independently reproduce every underlying measurement.

The property-check results, in-toto agreement, ECDSA cause split and
release-audit printed-table alignment remain recorded rather than
independently re-derived from the public package. Its dispositions omit the
programs, prompts, responses and truth needed for full cryptographic replay.
A separately staged full candidate contains those dependencies and re-executes
851 programs on public inputs in a sandbox; the syntax rejection is not
executed. These local checks agree with all 852 verdicts: 801 recoveries,
20 correct abstentions and 31 rejections. They run without hosted-model access
and verify programs, not regenerated model responses.
\texttt{verify.py} checks digests and missing full-lock dependencies.
Release judgments remain source-linked assessments. Policy regrading is
exploratory on the frozen cohort and measures a contract mismatch, not a
capability difference.

\textbf{Ethics considerations.} Targets are local, purpose-built fixtures;
no production or third-party system, personal data, or human subject is
involved. Experiment-generated escrow secrets are revealed only after a
verification attempt. The small safety probe reports refusals, not capability or
risk; the withdrawn composite score remains withdrawn.

\textbf{Generative AI usage.} Runs retain declared routes and budgets.
Prose and figures used AI assistance under author review. Retained records,
frozen analyses, and task-oracle verdicts support quantitative claims;
automatic checks establish specified arithmetic and identity links, not
scientific validity. AI-assisted audit judgments retain their diagnostic
limits and sources.
