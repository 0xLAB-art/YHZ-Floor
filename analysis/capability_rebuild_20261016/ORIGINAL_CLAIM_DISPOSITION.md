## Additions grounded in current uncommitted observations

The offline census reads all twelve final prospective records and their transcripts, all 74 historical raw transcripts and their copied counterparts, and seven source/result inputs: 179 pinned files. It checks 31 submitted-code digests, five accepted plaintext-digest relations, three ECDSA key/signature verdict pairs, 42 historical accepted candidate-digest relations, and 74 copy pairs. No provider call or submitted attack program is executed during this reanalysis.

The capability findings added to the narrative are four first-submission successes, four additional successes after feedback, six nonzero-exit CBC submissions before eventual recovery, and the per-session padding-query counts 20,737 / 20,994 / 21,508. The zero-plaintext stream generator is distinguished from what the retained task brief discloses. This is an information-condition finding; a controlled causal prompt ablation remains absent.

## Scientific limits carried forward

The prospective cohort remains one mutable access path with three sessions per task. There is no matched no-feedback, no-model construction, human, or cross-model arm. Fixed operations are feasibility controls. Historical provider identities and some runtime links are incomplete. Consequently the study cannot establish comparative reasoning quality, causal feedback effectiveness, general model rankings, unknown-vulnerability discovery or population-level exploit speed.
