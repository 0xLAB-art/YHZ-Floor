## Corrections and checks

- The preserved HEB public/private case tree has **118/118** target
  correspondences passing. The check compares the public target with the
  private `state`, including primitive-specific cryptographic fields, and
  checks the certificate target artifact hash against the private target hash.
  A display/name match alone is not treated as correspondence.

  private-only difference at `$.claims.reference_oracle_acceptance`; one pair
  has no JSON difference. These are reported differences, not silently treated
  as equivalent. The private certificate is not evidence that the public
  certificate's semantics are the same.
- Recomputed IAR-C preserved-pass counts use raw denominators: 44 total, 8
  malformed, 36 substantive, 20 substantive attack cases, 16 honest cases,
  2 honest full claims, and 10 eligible partial claims. Thus V2 false
  promotion is **10/20**; V2 full-claim false rejection is **0/2**; V2
  partial functionality is **N/A**, with raw partial rejection coverage
  **10/10**. V3-full partial acceptance is **10/10**. Malformed controls are
  excluded from substantive rates.
- Counts are derived from retained `systems` decisions plus `asserted` levels.
  Diagnostic `system_levels` and stored `level6_promotion` flags are not
  treated as verdicts. Missing/invalid retained decisions produce
  `INCONCLUSIVE`, not a zero.

## Missing evidence and limitations

- The utility verifies preserved bytes and recomputes row-level metrics; it
  does not prove that a historical generator was independent, that a provider
  was not called, or that a private registry is authentic.
- Public/private certificate differences are descriptive. No semantic
  equivalence claim is made without cryptographic-field comparison.
- Target hashes follow the preserved certificate/private metadata convention;
  the utility does not infer a new serialization or retroactively repair a
  historical hash convention.
- If no eligible partial cases exist, partial functionality is explicitly
  reported as `N/A`, never as zero success.
