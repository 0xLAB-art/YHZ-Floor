## Ranked answer

1. **C2b local hybrid-policy enforcement — PRIMARY_VERIFIED.** Supported: server group policy changed whether the classical-only client completed under the recorded causal contrast. Unproven: ML-KEM break, broad TLS weakness, or deployment prevalence.
2. **Fixed operation/verifier control — PRIMARY_VERIFIED.** Supported: RC4, CBC, ECDSA, and ChaCha operation paths each reached accepted oracle outputs in 3/3 local runs. Unproven: model/agent success or agent timing.
3. **A1 environment sensitivity — PRIMARY_BOUNDED.** Supported: WSL2 failed 3-sigma while VMware reported z=113.6987 with 90,000 valid trimmed samples. Unproven: causal environment attribution and portability.
4. **C2-T1 bounded wire-edit reproduction — PRIMARY_BOUNDED.** Supported: three open-pipeline runs achieved 3/3 wire validity and ASR. Unproven: AI necessity, toolchain generality, and binary-verification equivalence.
5. **D1 Spread-Table result — PRIMARY_BOUNDED.** Supported: retained record reports 16/16 bytes/cache lines and zero XOR difference after 200,000 samples. Unproven: raw-sample auditability, independent replication, portability, and general full-key recovery.
6. **Historical session populations — HISTORICAL_LIMITED.** Supported: retained mixed outcomes and failure states exist, including 74 sessions with 42 success, 2 failure, and 30 no-verdict events. Unproven: clean capability rate, independence of v2/v3 copies, and immutable provider attribution.

## Strongest omission

The main scientific omission is not necessarily a missing headline result. It is the absence of a complete repository-to-paper discovery map. The current paper selectively covers C2b, A1, and SP2-05 and intentionally excludes C2-T1, B1, and D1 from headline claims, but it does not expose the full nested inventory, smoke-control distinction, exact deduplication, or the fixed-operation rerun’s independent status. A reviewer could mistake selective scope for project-wide inventory closure.
